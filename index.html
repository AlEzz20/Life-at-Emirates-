TRANSPORT

Getting around without a car

Bus & Hafilat

Get one card each for Raihana, Hafa and Hafiz. Both children pay at ages 11 and 15. Tap in and out. The fare starts at AED 2 plus 5 fils per kilometre, capped at AED 5 per person on standard services. Start with pay-as-you-go and compare with the AED 95 monthly pass if anyone rides often.

Where to get a Hafilat card ↗

Compare taxi apps

  • Abu Dhabi Taxi for a silver metered taxi, or call 600 535 353.
  • Yango for the public taxi option where shown.
  • Bolt for a standard ride with up to four seats.
  • Careem and Uber to compare live quotes and offers.

A silver taxi starts at AED 12 minimum per car. Booking adds AED 4 by day or AED 5 at night. Hail a silver taxi at the roadside or mall rank to skip the booking fee.
